<idea>
**Design Movement:** Editorial Brutalism meets Data Journalism

**Core Principles:**
1. Raw typographic hierarchy — oversized display numbers and metrics dominate the visual field
2. Grid-breaking asymmetry — content bleeds into margins, columns of unequal weight
3. Data as art — growth numbers and charts are the hero visuals, not decorative

**Color Philosophy:**
- Off-white (#F5F2ED) background — warm, paper-like, not sterile
- Ink black (#0D0D0D) for primary type — maximum contrast
- Electric lime (#C6F135) as the single accent — data spikes, CTAs, hover states
- Muted slate (#8A8F99) for secondary text

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Horizontal rule-divided sections with full-bleed number callouts
- Case study cards that break the grid — offset by 20–30px from baseline
- Sticky left-rail navigation with section labels in small-caps

**Signature Elements:**
1. Giant percentage numbers (e.g., "+509%") rendered in a condensed display font, layered behind content
2. Thin horizontal rules with section timestamps in monospace
3. Metric "ticker" strip in the hero — scrolling key stats

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover reveals data context (e.g., hover "+509%" → tooltip shows before/after)
- Smooth scroll with section snap
- Number counter animations on scroll-into-view

**Animation:**
- On load: staggered fade-up for hero text (0.1s delay per line)
- Counters: count-up animation triggered by IntersectionObserver
- Cards: subtle 2px upward translate on hover with shadow deepening

**Typography System:**
- Display: Bebas Neue (condensed, bold) — for numbers and hero headline
- Body: DM Sans (400/500) — clean, modern, readable
- Mono: JetBrains Mono — for tool names, dates, metrics labels
</idea>

<idea>
**Design Movement:** Neo-Brutalist Growth Dashboard

**Core Principles:**
1. Unapologetic data display — metrics are the personality
2. Strong borders and hard shadows instead of soft drop-shadows
3. Colour blocking — sections alternate between dark and light backgrounds

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep navy (#0E1628) for hero and dark sections — authority, depth
- Warm cream (#FAF7F0) for light sections — approachable contrast
- Vivid amber (#F5A623) as accent — energy, growth, optimism
- White (#FFFFFF) for text on dark backgrounds

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width alternating dark/light section bands
- Case studies in a 2-column grid with hard-border cards
- Hero: left-aligned text, right side has an abstract data visualization (animated SVG lines)

**Signature Elements:**
1. Hard-border cards with amber top-border accent (4px solid)
2. Animated SVG growth line in hero background
3. Amber pill badges for industry tags (SaaS, Fintech, E-commerce)

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Bold hover states — cards lift with hard shadow offset
- CTA buttons with amber fill and navy text — high contrast, impossible to miss
- Section transitions feel like turning pages

**Animation:**
- Hero: SVG line draws itself on load (stroke-dashoffset animation)
- Counters: number roll-up on scroll
- Cards: translateY(-4px) + box-shadow increase on hover

**Typography System:**
- Display: Syne (geometric, bold) — modern and distinctive
- Body: Manrope (400/500/700) — clean, versatile
- Accent/labels: IBM Plex Mono — data-forward feel

## Selected Design: Neo-Brutalist Growth Dashboard (Option 3)

**Rationale:** This approach best reflects Rumi's professional identity as a data-driven SEO specialist. The bold metrics, growth-oriented visuals, and strong colour contrast will make her results impossible to ignore — exactly what a hiring manager needs to see in 30 seconds. The amber accent communicates energy and optimism, which suits the Australian job market's preference for confident, results-oriented candidates.
